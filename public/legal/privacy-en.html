NOVA — Privacy Policy

Updated: August 2026 · health.nova.app

1. Who we are

NOVA provides personal wellness insights — not medical diagnosis.

2. What we collect

  • Account info (email, name)
  • Watch health data with your permission (HR, HRV, sleep, activity)
  • Meal photos and calorie context
  • Blood test PDFs you upload
  • NOVA AI chat messages

3. How we use data

Personal baseline, change detection, explain-before-alert. We do not sell your data.

4. Security

Encrypted in transit and at rest. Access limited to your account.

5. Your rights

Request export or deletion: support@nova.health
